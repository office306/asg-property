What Is the BRR Strategy?

Published by ASG Property • Practical guide

BRR stands for Buy, Refurbish and Refinance. The strategy aims to improve a property's value and rental performance before refinancing it onto a longer-term product.

The basic stages

  1. Buy below or with room for improvement
  2. Complete a controlled refurbishment
  3. Let the property where appropriate
  4. Refinance against the improved value
  5. Retain the asset or recycle capital

Main risks

  • Refurbishment overspend
  • Overestimated end value
  • Finance delays
  • Rental demand assumptions
  • Unexpected legal or structural issues
