Know what you are paying

Rent, option payments and purchase credits explained.

The written agreements must clearly distinguish the payment for living in the property from the payments that may be credited if you complete the purchase.

£

Monthly rent

Rent pays for your occupation of the property. It does not build equity and is not deducted from the purchase price unless the agreement expressly states otherwise.

1

Upfront option payment

This secures the purchase option. Under the proposed ASG arrangement, 100% is credited towards the deposit or purchase price if you complete in accordance with the contract.

+

Monthly purchase top-up

This is paid separately from rent and accumulates as a purchase credit. It is applied on completion subject to the exact written terms.

Agreed purchase price

Fixed for the stated option period.

The agreement identifies the purchase price and the deadline for exercising the option. If the market rises, the agreed price may be beneficial; if the market falls, you could be agreeing to pay more than the later market value.
