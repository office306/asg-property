Investing in Huntingdon

Published by ASG Property • Practical guide

Huntingdon attracts investor interest because of transport connections, employment access and a broad mix of family housing and rental demand.

What to review

  • Street-level rental demand
  • Access to transport and employment
  • Property condition
  • Comparable sales and rents
  • Local licensing and planning requirements
  • Likely tenant profile

Area-wide averages are only a starting point. Individual streets and property types can perform very differently.
