How to Reduce Rental Voids

Published by ASG Property • Practical guide

Rental voids reduce annual income and often arrive with cleaning, advertising and re-letting costs.

Practical steps

  • Price the property realistically
  • Keep it clean, safe and well maintained
  • Start renewal conversations early
  • Respond quickly to repairs
  • Use clear photography and accurate listings
  • Consider a guaranteed-rent arrangement where suitable
