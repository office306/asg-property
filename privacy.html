Privacy Policy

ASG Property Ltd collects information submitted through this website and our Google Forms to respond to enquiries, assess requirements and maintain business records.

Tenant Buyer enquiries

If you request or complete a Tenant Buyer application, we may collect your name, contact details, current address, housing requirements, household size, employment information, available upfront funds and monthly affordability. We use this information to assess your enquiry, send the requested application and contact you about potentially suitable opportunities.

Storage and service providers

Enquiries may be processed and stored using Google Forms, Google Sheets and email services. Access is limited to people who need the information for the enquiry process. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your enquiry information, subject to any legal or record-keeping obligations. You may also ask us to stop contacting you about Tenant Buyer opportunities.
