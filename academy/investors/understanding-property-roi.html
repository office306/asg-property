Understanding Property ROI

Published by ASG Property • Practical guide

Return on investment compares profit or annual return with the cash committed to a deal.

Why the definition matters

Different investors calculate ROI differently. Some divide profit by total project cost, while others divide by cash invested. Always identify the method being used before comparing deals.

Do not use ROI alone

Also consider risk, time, liquidity, finance exposure, management requirements and the reliability of the assumptions.
