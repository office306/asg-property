Guaranteed Rent in Cambridgeshire and Huntingdon: Landlord FAQs Answered

ASG Property practical guide • Cambridgeshire & the East of England

If you own a rental property in Cambridgeshire, Huntingdon, St Neots or Peterborough, you may have come across the phrase “guaranteed rent” and wondered how it works in practice. What happens during voids? Who deals with maintenance? How long can the arrangement run? And is it suitable for your property?

This guide answers the questions landlords across the East of England ask us most, in plain language and without hype.

What is guaranteed rent?

Guaranteed rent is a rental model where the landlord receives an agreed rent for an agreed period, subject to the written terms of the arrangement. With ASG Property, suitable landlords may be offered up to five years of guaranteed rent.

The rent is agreed up front, helping give landlords greater certainty over monthly income during the agreed term.

How does guaranteed rent differ from a standard letting agent?

With a traditional letting arrangement, rental income depends on the property remaining occupied and the tenant continuing to pay. When a tenancy ends, a void period can mean lost income while a replacement tenant is found.

Under a guaranteed rent arrangement, the agreed rent continues during the contracted term even if the property is temporarily vacant, subject to the terms of the agreement.

What happens during void periods?

One of the biggest concerns for landlords is a property sitting empty while bills and other costs continue. Under ASG Property's guaranteed rent model, the agreed landlord payment continues during the contracted term, including periods when the property is not occupied, subject to the written agreement.

ASG Property takes responsibility for the day-to-day management and occupancy arrangements, giving landlords greater predictability over cash flow.

Who covers maintenance and repairs?

Maintenance support forms part of the arrangement. During the agreed term, smaller day-to-day maintenance matters can be handled for you, reducing the need to coordinate routine call-outs yourself.

Larger repairs, structural works and other responsibilities depend on the specific agreement and the nature of the issue. These responsibilities should always be clearly set out before the arrangement begins.

Do I still need to manage anything myself?

ASG Property's Landlord Solutions are designed to reduce day-to-day involvement. You have a single point of contact and one team coordinating the ongoing management of the arrangement.

For landlords who value predictable income and less hands-on management, this can provide a simpler way to hold a rental property.

How long can the guaranteed rent last?

For suitable properties and landlords, ASG Property can offer terms of up to five years. A longer term can make budgeting and income planning easier, whether the property is part of a wider portfolio or a single investment.

The exact term and rent level will depend on the property, location, condition and your circumstances, so these are assessed before anything is agreed.

Is this a rent-to-rent arrangement?

The structure and legal responsibilities depend on the agreement being offered. Rather than relying on labels, landlords should look closely at who is contracting with them, who is responsible for occupancy and management, how the rent is paid and what each party is responsible for.

ASG Property aims to keep the arrangement clear and transparent so you understand how it applies to your own property before committing.

Which areas does ASG Property cover?

ASG Property works with landlords and property owners across the East of England, including:

  • Cambridgeshire
  • Huntingdon
  • St Neots
  • Peterborough
  • Bedfordshire
  • Northamptonshire

If your property is in one of these areas, ASG Property can assess whether a guaranteed rent arrangement may be suitable.

I'm selling my property — could guaranteed rent still be relevant?

Potentially. If the timing of a sale is not right, the property needs some work, or you want it to produce income while deciding your next step, a managed rental arrangement may be worth considering.

Whether that is appropriate depends on your plans, timescale, property condition and the terms available.
