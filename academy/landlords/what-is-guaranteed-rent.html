What Is Guaranteed Rent?

Published by ASG Property • Practical guide

Guaranteed rent is an arrangement where a property company agrees to pay an agreed rent to a landlord for a defined term, subject to property suitability and a written contract.

How it differs from ordinary letting

With a traditional tenancy, the landlord's income may stop during void periods. Under a guaranteed-rent arrangement, the company usually remains responsible for the agreed rent during the term, provided the agreement remains in force.

Potential benefits

  • More predictable income
  • Reduced exposure to voids
  • One professional point of contact
  • Less day-to-day involvement

Points to check

  • The agreed rent and payment date
  • Length of the term
  • Repair and maintenance responsibilities
  • Inspection arrangements
  • Break clauses and end-of-term requirements
