Guaranteed Rent vs Traditional Letting

ASG Property practical guide

Traditional letting may offer more direct control, while guaranteed rent usually prioritises consistency and reduced management.

Key points

  • Compare headline rent with annual net income
  • Review void risk
  • Check management responsibilities
  • Read break clauses carefully
