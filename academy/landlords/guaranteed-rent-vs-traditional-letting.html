Guaranteed Rent vs Traditional Letting

Published by ASG Property • Practical guide

Traditional letting and guaranteed rent serve different landlord priorities. Traditional letting can offer more direct control, while guaranteed rent usually focuses on consistency and reduced management.

Traditional letting

  • Potential for full market rent
  • Direct landlord or agent control
  • Possible voids and changeover costs
  • More exposure to day-to-day issues

Guaranteed rent

  • Agreed income for an agreed term
  • Reduced void exposure
  • One contractual point of contact
  • Rent may be set below open-market headline rent in exchange for certainty
