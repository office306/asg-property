Investing in St Neots

Published by ASG Property • Practical guide

St Neots has strong commuter appeal and a wide range of family housing. Investors should assess purchase prices carefully against realistic rent and end value.

Key considerations

  • Station access and commuting demand
  • Family housing supply
  • Condition and refurbishment potential
  • Local rent ceilings
  • Exit demand from owner-occupiers
