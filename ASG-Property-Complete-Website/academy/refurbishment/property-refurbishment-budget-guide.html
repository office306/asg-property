Refurbishment

Property Refurbishment Budget Guide

Published by ASG Property • Practical guide

A refurbishment budget should be based on a defined scope rather than a single rough figure.

Common cost headings

  • Strip-out and waste
  • Electrics and plumbing
  • Heating
  • Kitchen and bathrooms
  • Plastering and decoration
  • Flooring
  • Windows, doors and roofing
  • External works
  • Professional fees and contingency

Contingency

Older or poorly maintained properties often reveal additional work after strip-out. A contingency should reflect the property's condition and how complete the survey information is.
