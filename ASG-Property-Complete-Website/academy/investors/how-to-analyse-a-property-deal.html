How to Analyse a Property Deal

Published by ASG Property • Practical guide

A good-looking purchase price does not automatically make a good property deal. Analysis should cover the complete cost and the realistic exit.

Core numbers

  • Purchase price
  • Stamp duty and legal costs
  • Refurbishment and contingency
  • Finance and holding costs
  • Expected rent
  • Realistic end value
  • Selling or refinance costs

Key outputs

Review profit, ROI, yield, cash invested, cash left in after refinance and sensitivity to lower values or higher costs.
